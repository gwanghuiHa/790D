Model & conventions

Coordinates: [x, x′, y, y′, z, δ], in m, rad and fractional momentum deviation. Positive z is toward the bunch head; positive δ is higher momentum. Positive quadrupole gradient focuses x. Dipoles use a sector-body map plus entrance/exit edge matrices, including dispersion, path length and a finite-fringe correction. The model assumes a 20 mm full pole gap and FINT = 0.5; ψ is calculated automatically for each face. Geometric edge angles remain independent. At zero edge angle, the finite-fringe term gives a small vertical defocusing kick. TDC uses a uniform distributed thick-cavity map, linearized at z = 0; κ is the zero-phase slope in m⁻¹, actual slope κ cos φ. The centroid kick is κ sin φ / kRF, with kRF = 2πf/(βc). Phase 0° is zero crossing. The corresponding longitudinal kick and κ²L/6 term are included. Velocity slip is included in drifts, quads, and bends; neglected within the ultrarelativistic TDC map. Slits are zero-length absorbing apertures. Screens are non-intercepting virtual diagnostics, including within elements.

RMS values are centered on the surviving beam centroid and use all surviving particles, including those outside the displayed image. Save/load preserves the random seed in instructor experiments. Student beamline files do not contain initial-beam parameters.
